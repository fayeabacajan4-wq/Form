My story

Built by people who love the craft

Form started as a stack of index cards on my desk. I just turned it into a tool.

The original index-card system, c. 2019

Why we built Form

Every project began the same way: a messy brainstorm, a dozen half-written emails, and a brief nobody fully agreed on. We thought there had to be a calmer way.

So we made one. Form walks you through the decisions that matter — style, structure, features — and hands you back a single page your developer can actually build from.

No jargon. No 40-slide decks. Just a thoughtful plan, the way it used to be done.

What we believe

The principles behind every Form brief.

✎

Clarity over cleverness

A brief should be read once and understood. We strip the fluff and keep the signal.

◷

Slow is fine

Good planning takes a few quiet minutes. We'd rather you think than rush.

⚑

Build for everyone

Every brief includes accessibility as a default, not an afterthought.

"A good plan today beats a perfect one next month."
